# Card doubts — for the owner to review

Each entry: card, the question, what was implemented meanwhile. Agents append here; keep entries short.

## Decisions taken by default (confirm or change)

- **Non-holo duplicates excluded** (Fossil #16–30; Team Rocket #18–31, #71, #72, #80), as in Jungle.
- **Dark Raichu (TR #83, "Rare Secret")** mapped to `CardRarity.RareHolo` (no secret rarity in the enum).
- **Starter decks and cosmetic skins** not changed for the new sets.
- **Mysterious Fossil in setup/mulligan**: treated as a Basic Pokémon ("play as if it were a Basic Pokémon").
- **Deck copy limit (mismatch to settle):** the frontend deck builder allows max 4 copies **by name across sets** (classic rule: Base Set Pikachu + Jungle Pikachu count together); the API validates max 4 **per card id**. The frontend is stricter, so nothing breaks today, but pick one rule (recommended: by name) and align the API.
- **Booster art for Fossil and Team Rocket is generated** (no scans were found): composites of the real logo + card art crops in `pokemon-tcg/public/packs/{fossil,team-rocket}/`. Replace with real pack images if you have them.
- **Power names:** Fossil/Team Rocket power names are in Spanish; Base Set/Jungle ones are still in English. Pick one convention.
- **Not done on purpose:** starter/theme decks, cosmetic skins for the new cards, landing page, game-core version bump (planned 0.6.0), merge, publish, deploy.

## Open questions

### Stage A (wrapping Base Set / Jungle trainers and powers — behavior kept unless noted)

- **Devolution Spray (BS #72)**: the printed card says "choose a Stage, *discard* all Evolution cards of that Stage or higher". The engine (and the catalog's Spanish text) removes ONE stage and returns it to the hand. Kept as is. Fixed meanwhile (unambiguous): the devolved Pokémon loses Special Conditions and attack effects ("just as if you had evolved it") and is Knocked Out when its damage ≥ the lower stage's HP (it used to stay in play with damage ≥ HP).
- **Energy Removal (BS #92)**: the printed card allows an Energy on *any* of the opponent's Pokémon; the engine and catalog text only allow the opponent's Active. Kept.
- **Super Energy Removal (BS #79)**: the catalog text says "the opponent's Active", the engine allows any opponent Pokémon (matches the printed card). Catalog text is the doubt.
- **Full Heal (BS #82)**: the engine refuses it when the Active has no condition (the card can be played anyway, to no effect), and it also removes Leer's "can't attack", which is not a Special Condition. Kept.
- **Poké Ball (JU #64)**: on heads the player "may" search; the registry requires choosing a Pokémon when the deck has one, and the deck is not shuffled when nothing is taken. Kept.
- **Peek (Mankey)**: "one of either player's Prizes" — the engine picks the prize at random instead of letting the player choose. Kept.
- **Damage Swap (Alakazam)**: one use = one move of N counters between two Pokémon (the API's `amount` semantics); the card moves 1 counter per use, as often as you like. Equivalent outcomes.

### Stage B (Fossil)

- **Mysterious Fossil (FO #62) — type**: the card has no type; in play it is a Pokémon with `types: []` and no Weakness/Resistance (Chain Lightning, Shift and Conversion see no type). Consumers that read `types[0]` must handle an empty list.
- **Mysterious Fossil — discard from play**: refused when it is your only Pokémon in play (instead of allowing it and losing). Discarding the Active Fossil leaves `playerNeedsToPromote` set (like Scoop Up); the turn continues after the promotion.
- **Mysterious Fossil — outside play it is a Trainer**: Poké Ball, Pokémon Trader, Revive, Pokémon Flute and Call for Family don't find it (they look for Pokémon cards); Item Finder, Lass, Computer Search, Recycle and Scavenge do. Kabuto/Omanyte/Aerodactyl on top of it give a Prize when Knocked Out; the Fossil under them goes to the discard pile as the Trainer.
- **Clefairy Doll (BS #70)**: was unplayable; it now uses the same data-driven rules as Mysterious Fossil (`playsAsBasic: { hp: 10 }`: 10 HP, no attacks, can't retreat, no Special Conditions, no Prize when Knocked Out, may be discarded from play). This is a Base Set behavior change (the card becomes playable).
- **Toxic Gas (Muk, FO #13)**: works from the Bench too and affects both players' Pokémon (other Toxic Gases excepted). Mysterious Fossil's rules are not a power and keep working. Charizard's Energy Burn auto-conversion inside `canUseAttack` only sees Toxic Gas when the caller passes the state.
- **Prehistoric Power (Aerodactyl, FO #1)**: works from the Bench and blocks both players (Evolution cards from hand and Pokémon Breeder). Devolution Spray is not "playing an Evolution card" and stays legal.
- **Clairvoyance (Omanyte, FO #52)**: works from the Bench; exposed as `isHandRevealed(state, side)` for consumers (the API must stop masking that hand).
- **Kabuto Armor (Kabuto, FO #50)**: halving is applied after Weakness/Resistance AND after PlusPower, Defender and Minimize (the text only says "after applying Weakness and Resistance"); it also halves bench damage (Kabuto on the Bench), self-damage of an attack and the damage of chosen bench targets, but not confusion damage (not "an attack doing damage").
- **Curse (Gengar, FO #5)**: a Knock Out caused by Curse gives the Prize automatically (like bench Knock Outs; `checkKnockouts`), and a Knocked Out Active leaves `opponentNeedsToPromote` set during the Curse user's turn (the opponent picks the new Active, then the turn continues).
- **Cowardice (Tentacool, FO #56)**: refused when Tentacool is your only Pokémon in play; returning the Active leaves `playerNeedsToPromote` (like Scoop Up). "The turn you put Tentacool into play" = `playedOnTurn` (setup counts as turn 0, so it can be used on turn 1).
- **Step In (Dragonite, FO #4)**: not a retreat (the retreat of the turn is still available); the Pokémon sent to the Bench loses Special Conditions and attack effects like any switch.
- **Transform (Ditto, FO #3)**: the copy keeps Ditto's name, id and power (only Transform: the copied card's own power is not gained) and takes the stage, HP, type, Weakness, Resistance, attacks and Retreat Cost of the opposing Active (it copies at all times while both are Active, not only "during an attack"). A Ditto facing another Ditto is a plain Ditto. Facing a Mysterious Fossil it becomes a 10 HP Pokémon without attacks, but still gives a Prize when Knocked Out. Double Colorless Energy counts as 2 Energy of any type. When the copy's HP drops below Ditto's damage (new Defending Pokémon, Ditto falls Asleep, Toxic Gas arrives) Ditto is Knocked Out right away (state-based `checkKnockouts` after attacks, trainers, powers, retreat, evolution, promotion and in endTurn), with the Prize taken automatically.
- **Transparency (Haunter, FO #6)**: implemented for the Defending Haunter only (a Benched Haunter hit by bench damage doesn't flip: bench targets are often chosen after the coins). The coin is part of the attack's coins (LAST in `coinResults`, see `getAttackCoinPlan`) and is flipped whenever the attack can do something to the Defending Pokémon (damage or an effect on it), even if the attack then does nothing (e.g. Twineedle tails/tails). Heads prevents damage and every effect on Haunter (like Agility); the attacker's own effects (self damage, bench damage) still happen.
- **Stone Barrage (Geodude, FO #47)**: coins are a run ending in the first tails (`attack: "untilTails"` in the coin plan); Metronome copying it does the same.
- **Thunderstorm (Zapdos, FO #15)**: coin i is for the i-th Benched Pokémon in bench order; self damage is 10 × tails (Kabuto Armor would halve none of it: Zapdos isn't Kabuto).
- **Gigashock (Raichu, FO #14)**: with 3 or fewer Benched Pokémon all are hit without asking; with more, exactly 3 must be chosen.
- **Prophecy (Hypno, FO #8)**: "look at up to 3" is implemented as the top 3 (or all if fewer); the attacker picks the deck, then the new order.
- **Wildfire (Moltres, FO #12)**: only Fire Energy cards (not Double Colorless) can be discarded; 0 is allowed; mills as many as the deck has.
- **Energy Conversion (Gastly, FO #33)**: the 2 Energy cards come from the discard pile as it was before the attack (Energy discarded by this attack's self-Knock Out can't be taken: the text recovers first, then does the damage).
- **Scavenge (Slowpoke, FO #55)**: the Psychic Energy discard is a cost (like Fire Spin, `selectedEnergyIds` / auto-pick); with no Trainer in the discard pile the attack still works.
- **Headache (Psyduck, FO #53)**: blocks every Trainer, including playing Mysterious Fossil / Clefairy Doll as a Basic. Not stopped by protection or Transparency (it affects the player, not the Defending Pokémon).
- **Clamp / Dive Bomb tails ("does nothing")**: no damage, no PlusPower, no effects; the attack counts as used and the turn ends.
- **Hyper Beam — Dragonair (BS #18) behavior change**: its "choose 1 Energy of the Defending Pokémon and discard it" was never applied by the engine (data only); it now uses the same pending attack choice as Golduck's Hyper Beam. Consumers must handle `pendingAttackChoice` (see Stage C).
- **Leech Life — Venonat (JU #63) fix**: healed a fixed 10; it now heals the damage actually done (after W/R; 0 if prevented), like Golbat/Zubat.
- **Mega Drain (Butterfree, JU #33)**: the printed card most likely says "(rounded up to the nearest 10)" like Absorb; the engine still rounds down (no source data for Jungle here). Kept; please confirm.
- **Agility-type protection vs Hurricane (Pidgeot, JU #8)**: fixed on the way — "prevent all effects of attacks" now also stops the return to the hand (it used to happen).
- **Transform while the other side has no Active** (after a Knock Out, before the promotion): Ditto is a plain Ditto meanwhile but is not Knocked Out by the HP change; the check runs once the new Active is promoted (and then uses the new copy).
- **Energy Search (FO #59)**: refused only with an empty deck; with no basic Energy in the deck it is played and finds nothing (the deck is shuffled). When there is one, picking it is required (no "fail to find").
- **Recycle (FO #61)**: any card of the discard pile (Energy included); playable with an empty discard pile (the coin is still flipped).
- **Gambler (FO #60)**: the Gambler itself is not shuffled in (it goes to the discard pile); with a short deck you draw what is left.
- **Mr. Fuji (FO #58)**: a Mysterious Fossil under a Kabuto goes back into the deck as the Trainer card.
- **Power names in Spanish**: the Fossil powers are named in Spanish (Poder Prehistórico, Gas Tóxico, Transformación, Entrada, Maldición, Transparencia, Comportamiento Extraño, Armadura Kabuto, Clarividencia, Cobardía) as asked, while Base Set / Jungle power names are still English (Rain Dance, Damage Swap...). `POWER_EFFECTS` is keyed by these names. Jungle #33–63 attack names are also still English.
- **Translations to review**: Liofilización (Freeze Dry), Gigadescarga (Gigashock), Bomba Sónica (Sonicboom), Fuego Salvaje (Wildfire), Bomba en Picado (Dive Bomb), Golpe Terrorífico (Terror Strike), Despiste (Spacing Out), Rebuscar (Scavenge), Baba Asquerosa (Nasty Goo), Lluvia de Piedras (Stone Barrage), Esconderse en el Caparazón (Hide in Shell), Entrada (Step In).

### Stage D (Team Rocket)

- **Rainbow Energy (TR #17)**: "counts as every type of basic Energy while in play" is applied to every "<type> Energy (card) attached" check: costs, discard costs, Water Gun / Hydrocannon bonus, Continuous Fireball's coin count, Fireball / Playing with Fire requirement, Wildfire, Final Beam's Water count, Energy Trans and Gather Fire (they can move it; no damage then). In the hand it is not a basic Energy (Energy Search, Energy Retrieval, Nightly Garbage Run, Rain Dance and Afternoon Nap don't take it). Its 10 damage is not attack damage (no Kabuto Armor, no protection) and can Knock Out (Prize for the opponent). When the engine picks Energy to discard (Ember auto-pick, Continuous Fireball, Fireball) printed-type cards go first, Rainbow last; the player doesn't choose.
- **Full Heal Energy (TR #81)**: keeps Leer's "can't attack" (not a Special Condition). **Potion Energy (TR #82)**: nothing when undamaged.
- **Special Energy in decks**: limited to 4 copies like any card (`isLimitedInDeck`); only basic Energy is unlimited. The API / deck builder must enforce it.
- **Summon Minions / Sneak Attack / Reel In**: trigger when the card is played from the hand (evolving, Pokémon Breeder; benching during the game for a future Basic), only if the power works at that moment (Toxic Gas / Goop Gas Attack stop it: no delayed trigger). Not from the deck (Rapid Evolution). Summon Minions: Mysterious Fossil is not a "Basic Pokémon" there (nor for Reel In); up to the Bench room; 0 allowed (deck still shuffled). Sneak Attack: damage with Dark Golbat's Weakness/Resistance but it is not attack damage (no PlusPower, Defender, Kabuto Armor, protection, Strikes Back).
- **Hay Fever (Dark Vileplume)**: works from the Bench and for both players; also stops playing Mysterious Fossil / Clefairy Doll.
- **Sticky Goo (Dark Muk)**: the +2 is added after Retreat Aid's reduction: max(0, printed − Retreat Aid) + 2.
- **Sinkhole (Dark Dugtrio)**: works from the Bench; one coin per working Dark Dugtrio of the opponent; the damage goes to the Pokémon that retreated (already on the Bench) and can Knock It Out (Prize for the Dugtrio's owner, taken at once). Only real retreats (not Switch, Step In, Scoop Up...). If the caller passes no coins the engine flips them (Math.random).
- **Final Beam (Dark Gyarados)**: only for the Defending Dark Gyarados Knocked Out by the attack's damage to it (main damage or a chosen-target attack), not by bench damage (like Transparency, the coin is part of the attack's coins and is flipped whenever the attack can damage it; it is ignored without a Knock Out). The power must work before the attack (Asleep → no coin). Damage = 20 × Water Energy cards with the attacker's Weakness/Resistance; not reduced by Kabuto Armor / Defender (not attack damage).
- **Frenzy (Dark Primeape)**: +30 is added to the base damage (before Weakness/Resistance) when the attack does damage, and to confusion damage (20 + 30). Confusion doesn't stop it; Toxic Gas / Goop Gas do.
- **Trickery (Rattata)**: the Prize is chosen by position (face down) unless Here Comes Team Rocket! was played. Needs Prizes and a non-empty deck.
- **Evolutionary Light / Matter Exchange**: Evolutionary Light can be used with no Evolution card in the deck (searches, finds nothing, shuffles); Matter Exchange needs a card in the deck.
- **Long-Distance Hypnosis / Pollen Stench**: need both Active Pokémon; heads: Thick Skinned and Mysterious Fossil stop it; tails affects your Active (Drowzee / Dark Gloom itself if Active). Agility-type protection doesn't stop a power.
- **Stare (Dark Arbok)**: the chosen Pokémon's power stops (`powersSuppressed` with its id) unless it was Knocked Out or the Defending Pokémon is under Agility/Transparency. A Benched Pokémon can be chosen.
- **Chosen-target damage (Stare, Flitter, Dig Under, Coin Hurl, Flame Pillar, Drag Off)**: PlusPower and Minimize only when the target is the Active; Defender on the target wherever it is (also Benched, since the cross-set combo fixes — "damage done to that Pokémon by attacks"); Kabuto Armor and Invisible Wall always; Strikes Back, Mirror Shell, Destiny Bond and Final Beam react. A Knocked Out Active gives its Prize at once (no manual pick) and the opponent promotes before the turn ends. Coin Hurl: the coin is flipped before choosing (same odds).
- **Drag Off (Dark Machoke)**: the switch happens even if the old Defending Pokémon is protected by Agility (the effect is on the Benched Pokémon); the switched-out Pokémon loses its conditions; the new one takes 20 with W/R.
- **Fling (Dark Machamp)**: Agility / Transparency stop it; Mysterious Fossil goes into the deck as the Trainer.
- **Knock Down / Bench Manipulation**: the attack's coins are flipped by the defending player (`attackFlipper: "opponent"`); the server rolls them like any coin.
- **Continuous Fireball (Dark Charizard)**: one coin per Fire Energy *card* (Rainbow counts, Double Colorless doesn't).
- **Mass Explosion (Dark Weezing)**: counts both sides including itself and the Defending Pokémon; the second part hits Dark Weezing itself, every named Benched Pokémon and a named Defending Pokémon (added to the main damage, prevented with it). Ditto counts by its own name.
- **Energy Bomb (Dark Electrode)**: one choice per Energy card (Buzzap energy included). **Third Eye (Dark Golduck)**: discarding is mandatory, then 0–3 cards.
- **Mirror Shell (Dark Wartortle)**: the damage done back has no Weakness/Resistance and isn't halved by Kabuto Armor; only while Dark Wartortle is the Active (it is cleared on the Bench / evolving).
- **Rapid Evolution (Magikarp)**: allowed on the first turn and on a Magikarp played this turn (only playing Evolution cards from the hand has those limits); Prehistoric Power stops it (no search); the card must be taken when there is one; Special Conditions end ("counts as evolving"); no on-play power.
- **Vanish (Abra)**: allowed with an empty Bench (the player then has no Pokémon and loses).
- **Petal Whirlwind (Dark Vileplume)**: the source text says "Flip a coins"; implemented as 3 coins (printed card).
- **Surprise Thunder (Dark Raichu)**: always 2 coins in the plan; the second is ignored after a tails.
- **Here Comes Team Rocket!**: playable when Prizes are already face up (no effect). **Rocket's Sneak Attack**: playable with an empty opponent's hand.
- **Challenge!**: the "face down" placement isn't modelled: the player chooses first, then the opponent (consumers must not show the player's choice to the opponent before both are done). A side with a full Bench gets an empty step. Mysterious Fossil isn't a "Basic Pokémon card" here. Declined (or both Benches full): the player draws 2.
- **Digger**: coins alternate (you, opponent, you...) in one "untilTails" list; the damage isn't attack damage (no W/R, no Kabuto Armor) and can Knock Out your own Active.
- **Nightly Garbage Run**: when there is something to choose at least 1 card must be chosen; playable with nothing (no effect). Mysterious Fossil and special Energy aren't eligible.
- **Goop Gas Attack**: also stops Toxic Gas and every passive power (Hay Fever, Sticky Goo...), both sides.
- **Sleep!**: Thick Skinned stops it; Agility doesn't (not an attack).
- **AI (Stage D polish)**: never plays Challenge! (the human would have to answer a Trainer step that no consumer relays for an AI play; enabling it needs an engine pending Trainer choice + a frontend flow), Digger or Here Comes Team Rocket!. Strange Behavior keeps Slowbro at ≥ 20 HP; Mr. Fuji only for a Benched Pokémon without Energy that lost half its HP; Imposter Oak's Revenge only against 6+ cards. Thresholds are guesses — tune freely.
- **Event wording (Stage D polish)**: Fossil / Team Rocket log lines changed to perspective-free Spanish ("Challenge!: Abra a la Banca de quien lo jugó", "Digger: Onix (Activo del otro jugador) recibió 10 de daño", "¡El defensor no podrá jugar cartas de Entrenador…!"). Wording to review.
- **Grimer's Poison Gas (TR #57)**: the source text says "Asleep" (not Poisoned); implemented as printed in the source.
- **Translations to review (Stage D)**: power names Invocar Secuaces, Socavón, Ataque Furtivo, Rayo Final, Recoger (Reel In), Fiebre del Heno, Luz Evolutiva, Hedor de Polen, Intercambio de Materia, Baba Pegajosa, Frenesí, Reunir Fuego, Hipnosis a Distancia, Engaño; attacks Teletransporte Explosivo, Choque Mental, Mirada, Revoloteo, Derribar, Lanzamiento (Fling), Líneas Magnéticas, Explosión Masiva, Cavar (Dig Under), Lanzamonedas, Arrastrar (Drag Off), Empujón (Knock Back), Coz (Rear Kick), Golpe de Cola (Tail Strike, distinct from Dratini's "Golpe Cola"), Cola Ígnea (Fire Tail), Salpicadura (Flop), Somnífero (Sleep Powder), Gas Venenoso (Poison Gas, Grimer and Koffing).
- **Base / Jungle / Fossil behavior changes in Stage D**: Energy selection labels show special Energy by name ("Double Colorless Energy" instead of "Energía incolora"); bench Strikes Back no longer triggers for Machamps that Mass Explosion's name filter skipped; a coin ForceSwitch (Fascinate) isn't applied by the legacy positional executeAttack (no coins); endTurn now ends Goop Gas / Stare before its Knock Out check (Ditto fix).

### Cross-set combo hunt (2026-10-11)

Five agents tested card combinations across the 4 sets (≈380 new tests, ≈133k explore-bot games). Fixed as clear rule bugs: Toxic on an already Poisoned Pokémon (now 20); retreat refused after attacking / during pauses / off-turn; Buzzap Energy paying attack costs; Energy Burn on the Bench never ending; Damage Swap able to KO a transformed Ditto; Rain Dance on Shift-Water Venomoth / Water-copying Ditto; Thick Skinned off while Snorlax is already Asleep/Confused/Paralyzed; PlusPower / Defender attached to a Pokémon (kept on evolution/devolution, PlusPower not moving with Switch, leaving play with their Pokémon, Defender reducing bench damage); PlusPower not adding when W/R bring damage to 0; Water Gun family not counting Water that pays the Colorless cost; Leech Seed not healing when damage is prevented; bench Strikes Back only for damaged Machamps; Metronome leaving a corrupted card; Swords Dance lasting to Scyther's next turn and not stacking. Open questions:

- **Retreating while Confused**: the original rulebook asks for a coin (tails: retreat fails). The engine lets a Confused Pokémon retreat normally. Add the coin?
- **Normal Poison on a Toxic-poisoned Pokémon**: keeps 20. A later ruling may make the new Poison replace Toxic (10).
- **Asleep + Poisoned Ditto between turns**: the Poison KO is checked with the HP before the Sleep coin.
- **Pokémon Breeder on Mysterious Fossil** (Kabutops / Omastar straight onto the Fossil): allowed. Confirm.
- **Evolutionary Light under Prehistoric Power**: the search is allowed; the card found can't be played.
- **Hurricane (Pidgeot) + Defender**: the card returns "all cards attached" to the hand; Defender now goes to the discard instead. Should it go to the hand?
- **Item Finder**: can it take back a Trainer discarded as its own cost? The engine says no.
- **Buzzap**: the opponent picks the Prize manually (Curse, Sneak Attack, Sinkhole and bench KOs take it automatically); Energy Trans excludes a Buzzap Electrode but Gather Fire / Energy Removal include it; a discarded Electrode keeps its Buzzap Energy data (no rule effect today; a single "Energy leaves play" helper would clean it).
- **Once-per-turn powers after Devolution Spray**: the devolved card keeps its id, so it can't reuse a power already used that turn.
- **Energy Burn / Shift when Toxic Gas or Goop Gas arrives later in the same turn**: the conversion already applied stays.
- **Metronome copying Water Gun / Hydro Pump**: the copied cost is ignored, so every Water Energy on Clefairy counts as extra.
- **Invisible Wall / Harden** are checked after PlusPower, Defender and Kabuto Armor; **Leech Seed** still heals when Resistance (not prevention) brings damage to 0.
- **Sudden death** isn't modelled: when both players lose at once the result depends on the order (kept as a skipped test).
- **AI damage estimates** ignore PlusPower, Kabuto Armor, Conversion-changed W/R and Swords Dance (play quality only).

### Gym Heroes (Pokémon catalog, 2026-10-16)

The 91 Pokémon's attacks run on effect data (new kinds tabled at the top of `test/gym-heroes-attacks.test.ts`); their 11 Pokémon Powers are in `test/gym-heroes-powers.test.ts` (judgment calls in "Gym Heroes Pokémon Powers" below). Every image URL answers 200 (pkmncards keeps the dot: `lt.-surges-…`, `sabrinas-mr.-mime-…`, and has no set code in Gym Heroes file names). Judgment calls on ambiguous text:

- **Crosscounter (Rocket's Hitmonchan)**: "attacks your opponent's Active Pokémon for double that amount" — no Weakness / Resistance applied (the text doesn't say; Fire Wall says "Apply Weakness and Resistance" and gets them). Its coin is the attack's defender coin, flipped only when the attack can damage it.
- **Shadow Images (Rocket's Scyther)**: the coin (flipped by the attacking player, `plan.defenderFlipper: "attacker"`) is only asked when the attack can damage the Defending Scyther; "until it takes damage" = until an attack does damage to it (confusion self-damage and Poison don't end it). Chosen-target attacks on it use the same coin.
- **Fairy Power (Erika's Clefable)**: the Active Pokémon is only offered when the Bench isn't empty (returning your last Pokémon would lose the game); returning everything still loses.
- **Lava Burst (Blaine's Magmar)**: counts basic Fire Energy cards only (Rainbow Energy isn't a "Fire Energy card" outside play). **Discharge** counts / discards a Rainbow Energy attached as Lightning (it provides Lightning in play). **Charge** takes basic Lightning Energy cards. **Electric Current** moves a basic Lightning Energy before a Rainbow one.
- **Crystal Beam (Misty's Tentacool)**: blocks attaching Energy cards to that Pokémon (from the hand, Rain Dance included); powers that move Energy already in play (Energy Trans, Gather Fire, Energy Charge) still can.
- **Deflector (Erika's Exeggcute)**: halves every attack damage done to it during the opponent's next turn (bench damage and its own self-damage included, through the Kabuto Armor hook); Poison isn't halved.
- **Sleight of Hand (Sabrina's Mr. Mime)** must take as many basic Energy as cards were put back when the deck has them; **Moonwatching** must take one if there is one (a search may "fail" by the rules; kept strict so the AI doesn't waste them). **Call for Friend** may take none.
- **Swift (Misty's Staryu)**: ignores Weakness, Resistance, Defender, Minimize-like reductions, Kabuto Armor / Deflector, protection (Agility, Harden…), Invisible Wall, Transparency and Shadow Images; PlusPower and Vermilion City Gym still add. Shell Armor and Strange Barrier don't reduce it either; Pollen Defense, Restless Sleep and Flee still happen (they don't change its damage).
- **Pain Amplifier (Sabrina's Gengar)**: damage counters, not damage (Defender, halving and barriers don't apply); only what prevents every effect of the attack on the Defending Pokémon (Agility, Transparency) stops it there.
- **Tunneling / Screaming Headbutt**: the "next turn" lock ends if the Pokémon is Benched or evolves (like other effects of attacks).
- **Focus Energy (Lt. Surge's Rattata)**: modelled like Swords Dance (+20 to its next attack = Gnaw's base 20 doubled).
- **Spanish names**: Base Set / Jungle left some attack names in English (Stomp, Drill Peck, Horn Attack, Razor Leaf, Call for Friend, Aurora Beam, Mega Drain); Gym Heroes translates them (Pisotón, Pico Taladro, Cornada, Hoja Afilada, Llamar a un Amigo, Rayo Aurora, Megaagotar) instead of reusing the English name. Lariat keeps its name.
- **Base Set Mewtwo's Psychic** (`Médium`, "+10 per Energy card on the Defending Pokémon") has no effect data; Erika's Exeggcute's Psychic now uses `bonusDamageSource: "defenderEnergyCards"`, which Mewtwo could reuse.
- **Vermilion City Gym**: no Lt. Surge's Pokémon has a chosen-target attack, so its +10 (normal damage step only) never misses one.

### Gym Heroes Pokémon Powers (2026-10-16)

- **Powers asked in the middle of an attack (Flee, Bench Guard)**: their owner decides after seeing the damage but before the attack does anything else, so `executeAttack` stops before applying anything and returns the state it was given with `pendingAttackChoice.reaction` (a normal step with `chooser`, `answerStepForAI` / the simulator answer it); `resolveAttackChoice` runs the attack again with the same coins and the answer (Metronome too). The deprecated positional `executeAttack` form never asks (the powers aren't used).
- **Flee (Misty's Tentacruel)**: only for the opponent's attack damaging the Defending Tentacruel through the normal damage step (not chosen-target damage such as Stare, nor its own self-damage); not offered when the damage Knocks it Out or with an empty Bench. Fleeing keeps the damage and prevents every other effect on it (Special Conditions, Whirlwind-style switches, Hurricane, Pain Amplifier's counters, Hyper Beam's discard...); the switch happens after the attack, so that attack's bench damage doesn't hit it on the Bench. "Already Asleep/Confused/Paralyzed" = before the attack.
- **Bench Guard (Brock's Rhydon)**: one question per Bench and attack listing every other Benched Pokémon that took 10 or more (the "once for each of them" clause): bench damage effects, Bench damage the attacker chooses and chosen-target attacks (Stare) on a Benched Pokémon; the attacker answers it for its own Bench (Selfdestruct). The 10 goes to Rhydon as is (no Weakness / Resistance or reductions on Rhydon) and may Knock it Out; Knock Outs are checked after the answer. With two Rhydons only the first working one is offered. Damage by powers (Sneak Attack) and damage counters (Curse, Pain Amplifier) aren't attack damage here and don't ask.
- **Pollen Defense (Erika's Vileplume)**: the attack's defender coin (after Final Beam in `defenderCoinKind`; flipped by Vileplume's owner), only when the attack can damage it and used only if it did (Knocked Out included); heads Confuses the attacking Pokémon (Thick Skinned / Trainers in play are immune). Swift doesn't skip it. Bench damage to a Benched Vileplume doesn't trigger it.
- **Rebirth (Rocket's Moltres)**: "you may" is always yes (returning it has no downside); every Knock Out path (attack, Bench, checkup, Strikes Back...) sends the other cards to the discard pile and the Moltres card to the hand, the Prize is still taken. The simulator's prize rule counts it as a Knock Out by its log line.
- **Shell Armor (Misty's Cloyster)**: "you may" is always yes. −10 on every damage done to it by an attack (Benched too, its own attacks' self-damage too), at the Kabuto Armor step (after Weakness / Resistance, PlusPower and Defender); not confusion damage.
- **Restless Sleep (Rocket's Snorlax)**: 20 to the attacking Pokémon without Weakness / Resistance (like Strikes Back: the text doesn't apply them); Asleep is checked before the attack; Toxic Gas stops it, the status doesn't.
- **Strange Barrier (Erika's Dratini)**: compared with the damage after PlusPower / Defender (like Invisible Wall), Benched too. "A Basic Pokémon" is the attacker's printed stage (a transformed Ditto and Metronome's Clefairy are Basic).
- **Photosynthesis (Erika's Oddish)**: every Energy card attached provides Grass only (Double Colorless provides 2 Grass), through `energyProvidesType(card, type, holder, state)` and the attack cost check; so Energy Trans can take any Energy card from it and Gather Fire / Energy Charge can't take a Fire / Lightning card from it. Without a state (`canUseAttack` called without one) Toxic Gas isn't seen.
- **Energy Charge (Lt. Surge's Magneton)**: any number of times; a Rainbow Energy on another Pokémon counts as a Lightning Energy card.
- **Fragrance Trap (Erika's Victreebel)**: offered only when the opponent has Benched Pokémon (the flip would do nothing otherwise); the switched Active loses its Special Conditions like any switch. The smart AI doesn't use it.
- **Natural Healing (Blaine's Vulpix)**: works from the Bench (the text sets no position).
